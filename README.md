# Gözde Özsezer, PhD

Assistant Professor | Public Health Nursing | Health AI & Data Analytics

I am an Assistant Professor in Nursing at Çanakkale Onsekiz Mart University, Türkiye, with a PhD in public health nursing and ongoing doctoral studies in bioengineering. My research connects public health nursing, machine learning, digital health, and interpretable prediction models.

## Research interests
- Trustworthy machine learning and external validation in healthcare
- Wearable sensor analytics and physiological time series
- Explainable AI, model calibration, and algorithm monitoring
- Public health inequalities and data-driven prevention

## Selected research directions
- **Wearable stress prediction:** participant-level validation, uncertainty and selective prediction (work in progress).
- **Mental health and music listening:** exploratory machine learning study (accepted manuscript; code release subject to data and author permissions).
- **Nursing AI safety:** postdeployment algorithm monitoring and clinical governance.
